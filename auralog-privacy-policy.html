AuraLog Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 12, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to AuraLog, a personal mood tracking and journaling mobile application. AuraLog is designed to help you capture, visualize, and reflect on your daily emotional states, thoughts, and reflections.

I built AuraLog as a personal project. This page is used to inform users regarding my policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information if anyone decides to use the AuraLog application.

Information Collection and Use

AuraLog is an offline-first application. Your privacy is my highest priority. Here is what you need to know about your data:

  • No Server Uploads: All logs, diary entries, mood scores, titles, and details you input into the app are stored exclusively on your device's local database. We do not host, store, or transmit your private diary entries to any external servers.
  • No Personal Identification: AuraLog does not require you to create an account, log in, or provide any personal details (such as your name, email address, or phone number) to use the app.
  • Telemetry & Usage Data: The app does not collect telemetry, analytical event tracking, or performance statistics about your usage.

Permissions

To provide optional convenience features, AuraLog may request the following device permissions:

  • Notifications: To send you daily gentle reminders to log your mood and maintain your journaling habit. You can disable these at any time via your device's settings.
  • Storage/Media Access: If the app supports locally exporting/importing backup files of your journal entries, we request access to save the backup file to your preferred local folder.

Third-Party Services

AuraLog does not integrate with third-party tracking, advertising (such as Google AdMob), or analytics SDKs (such as Firebase Analytics) that monitor your activity. Your journal remains 100% private to you.

Security

Because all of your data is stored locally on your device, the security of your journal relies on the safety of your hardware. I recommend protecting your device with a secure passcode, PIN, or biometric lock to prevent unauthorized access to your logs.

Links to Other Sites

This service does not contain links to external websites. If external links are added in the future (e.g. references to resources), we encourage you to review their respective privacy policies as we have no control over third-party content.

Children's Privacy

These services do not address anyone under the age of 13. I do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. Since no data is collected by the application, parents can rest assured that children's diaries remain completely offline and private on the local device.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

I may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. I will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about my Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact me at algochets@gmail.com.
